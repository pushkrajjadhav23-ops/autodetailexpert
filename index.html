Car Care Tips

Small habits that protect the finish between visits.

01

Wash with two buckets

Keep one bucket for soap and one for rinsing your wash mitt. Rinsing between passes stops grit from being dragged back across the paint — one of the main causes of fine swirl marks.

02

Skip the household detergent

Dish soap and general-purpose cleaners strip wax and sealant along with the dirt. A pH-neutral car shampoo cleans just as well without leaving the paint bare.

03

Dry with a clean microfiber towel

Dragging a rough or dirty cloth across the surface is how most swirl marks actually happen. Blot dry instead of wiping in long strokes.

FAQ

Questions we get most.

How long does ceramic coating take?

A full correction-and-coating job typically takes 2–3 days, since the paint needs time to cure under controlled conditions before the car leaves the bay.

Will paint correction remove deep scratches?

It removes swirl marks, light scratches, and oxidation in the clear coat. Deeper scratches that reach the base colour need touch-up paint first — we'll flag this during inspection.

Do I need to book in advance?

Inspections and basic washes are walk-in friendly. Coating and PPF slots are booked in advance since each job takes the full bay for multiple days.

What does the warranty actually cover?

Coating and PPF warranties cover failure of the product itself — peeling, yellowing, or loss of hydrophobic properties. They don't cover new stone chips or accident damage.
